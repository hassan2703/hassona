# Content guide

How to prepare photographs and films for the site, and exactly what is still missing.

## 1. The portfolio content checklist

Tick these off as they go into the Studio. Until then the site shows labelled placeholders.

### Photography

- [ ] **Opening frame**: the Saint Catherine photograph of the two guides walking toward the mountains. Full resolution.
- [ ] **Saint Catherine**: 8 to 15 best frames from the 2022 hike, plus 2 or 3 sentences of the story in your words.
- [ ] **Kérastase Elixir launch (2024, Salah El-Din Citadel)**: 8 to 15 best frames from the 20 to 30 you have. Confirm the credit line ("L'Oréal · Kérastase"?) and that you may name the client.
- [ ] **Cairo Streets** (or your own title): 8 to 15 frames.
- [ ] **Structures** (architecture, or your own title): 8 to 15 frames.
- [ ] **Faces** (portraits, or your own title): 8 to 15 frames.
- [ ] **Events**: a series if you have one beyond Kérastase. Optional.
- [ ] A film-only series if you have one. Optional, but your film work is a strong differentiator.

Minimum to launch: the opening frame plus **three** finished series. Goal: five to seven.

### Film

- [ ] **Showreel**, 60 to 90 seconds (brief below).
- [ ] **Film 1, 2 and 3**: the master files, a title for each, the year, your role on each (director, DP, editor), the client if any.
- [ ] A poster frame for each film (a still exported from the edit is perfect).

### About you

- [ ] **Portrait** of you.
- [ ] **Short bio** (2 or 3 sentences) and **your story** (2 short paragraphs). The current text is a draft written from your interview answers.
- [ ] **Clients list**: currently L'Oréal, Kérastase and Elle. Add any others you're allowed to name. Should Elle have a project, or stay as a name?
- [ ] **CV as a PDF**.
- [ ] **Cal.com link**: the part after `cal.com/`, for example `hassanbadawy/intro`. Until it's added, "Book a call" leads to the contact page.

## 2. Organising your files

Use one Google Drive folder with one sub-folder per series:

```
Hassan Badawy · Portfolio/
  00 Opening frame/
  01 Saint Catherine/
      01.jpg  02.jpg  ...       numbered in the order you want them shown
      notes.txt                  the story, captions, anything you remember
  02 Kerastase Elixir/
  03 Cairo Streets/
  04 Structures/
  05 Faces/
  10 Films/
      Showreel/   master file, poster.jpg, notes.txt
      Film 1/     ...
  20 About/
      portrait.jpg  cv.pdf  bio.txt
```

Name files by order (`01.jpg`, `02.jpg`), not by camera number, so the sequence survives.

### Choosing frames

- **Fewer is stronger.** 10 great frames beat 40 good ones. If you hesitate about a frame, cut it.
- **Open and close strong.** The first frame is the cover; the last is what people remember.
- **Vary the rhythm.** Mix wide and close, colour and black and white, busy and quiet. The row layouts (full, pair, silence, offset, sequence) are there to give each series a pace.

## 3. Exporting photographs

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Format | JPEG, quality 90 to 95 |
| Colour | sRGB |
| Size | 3000 to 4000 px on the long edge |
| File size | Under about 8 MB each |
| Metadata | Keep it: the Studio can read camera data |

Don't crop to fit the site. Every frame is shown in its original shape. In the Studio, set the **hotspot** (focal point) on covers so phones crop around the right spot.

Write a one-sentence **description** for every image. It is read aloud to blind visitors and helps Google understand the work. Describe what is in the frame, for example "Two local guides walking along a ridge in Saint Catherine at dusk".

## 4. Exporting films

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Upload to | The **Video** field of the Film in the Studio (Mux) |
| Master | H.264 or H.265 MP4, or ProRes, at the resolution you shot (4K is fine) |
| Bit rate | High. Mux re-encodes for every screen, so give it the best source |
| Audio | Stereo, AAC or PCM, mixed to around -14 LUFS |
| Poster | A still JPEG exported from the edit, same shape as the film |

If you ever stop using Mux, a compressed MP4 (under about 50 MB) can go in the **Video file** field instead.

## 5. Showreel brief

**Length:** 60 to 90 seconds. Shorter is better than padded.

**Shape:** 2.39:1 letterbox if most of your footage can take it; otherwise 16:9. Match the Film's **Shape** setting in the Studio.

**Structure:**

1. **Cold open, 3 to 5 seconds.** One shot that stops people. Something slow and full of light, close to the long-exposure feeling of the site. No logo, no title.
2. **Act one, about 20 seconds: your eye.** Personal footage: Sinai, Cairo streets, architecture. Let shots breathe. Cut on light and movement, not on the beat.
3. **Act two, about 25 seconds: what you deliver.** Commissioned work: Kérastase, events, people. More energy, faster cutting, faces and reactions.
4. **Act three, about 15 seconds: range.** Short shots from everything else, building pace.
5. **Close, 3 to 5 seconds.** Black. Your name in white: "Hassan Badawy · Photographer & Director". Hold, then cut.

**Rules:**

- Only licensed music. Atmospheric and patient fits the site better than trailer drums.
- Your best five seconds go first, not last.
- Every shot earns its place. If a shot only fills time, cut it.
- Mark a calm 4-second moment and set it as **Hover preview starts at** in the Studio; that loop plays silently when people hover over the reel.
